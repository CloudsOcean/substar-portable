# Target Japanese

- Produce concise, idiomatic Japanese subtitles; do not preserve source-language word order mechanically.
- Use particles, predicate endings, tense and politeness consistently and conservatively.
- Omit an understood subject only when context remains unambiguous; never invent identity or gender.
- Keep a modifier before its noun and place predicate information naturally, often toward the end of the Japanese clause.
- Do not strand particles, auxiliary predicates, connective endings or sentence-final elements.
- Preserve names, numbers, units, negation, condition, comparison and modality exactly once.
- Every target Cue must obey the active Japanese `hard_limit` and `count_rule`; never exceed it when a natural redistribution across Cues is possible.

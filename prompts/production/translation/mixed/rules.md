# Mixed-language source policy

- Treat code-switching as source meaning, not noise. Translate each span according to its actual language and the configured target language.
- Preserve the identity of names, brands, abbreviations, model numbers, URLs, quoted foreign wording and terms marked `do_not_translate`.
- Do not translate the same fact twice merely because it is repeated or paraphrased across two source languages.
- Do not silently discard a source-language span, and do not copy it unchanged unless target-language policy or the glossary permits that form.
- Use the whole request block to resolve which language a short ambiguous fragment belongs to.
- Keep language switches, quotations and metalinguistic references distinguishable when the speaker is discussing the foreign wording itself.

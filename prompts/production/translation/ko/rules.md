# Target Korean

- Produce concise, idiomatic Korean subtitles; do not preserve source-language word order mechanically.
- Use particles, predicate endings, tense, speech level and honorific force consistently and conservatively.
- Omit an understood subject only when context remains unambiguous; never invent identity or gender.
- Keep attributive clauses before their nouns and place predicate information naturally toward the clause end.
- Do not strand particles, connective endings, auxiliary predicates or sentence endings.
- Preserve names, numbers, units, negation, condition, comparison and modality exactly once.
- Every target Cue must obey the active Korean `hard_limit` and `count_rule`; never exceed it when a natural redistribution across Cues is possible.

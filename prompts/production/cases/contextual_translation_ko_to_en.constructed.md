# Contextual translation Korean→English contrast cases

1. `지역 기술자들이 제안한 방안을 / 위원회가 / 승인했습니다`. Weak: preserve object–subject–verb order; Preferred: `The committee approved / the plan / proposed by local engineers.` `N:M, full`.
2. `다리가 위험하다고 / 말한 것은 아니고 / 추가 점검이 필요하다고만 밝혔습니다`. Preferred: `They did not say the bridge was unsafe / they only said / another inspection was needed.` Preserve the contrastive negation. `N:M, partial`.
3. Recover an English subject only from context. Do not infer gender, identity or hierarchy from omitted Korean arguments or speech level.

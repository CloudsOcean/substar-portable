These examples demonstrate boundary policy only. Never copy their words or facts.

- Keep a mixed proper name together: `We launched / 微信支付 overseas`, not `We launched 微信 / 支付 overseas`.
- Do not split merely at a language switch: `请打开 Google Maps / 然后搜索这个地址` is valid when the first span is one complete instruction step.
- Preserve local dependencies: do not strand English articles or prepositions, Chinese particles, Japanese particles, or Korean case markers and endings.
- A clear speaker change, complete clause, independent reply or strong pause remains a preferred boundary regardless of script.

# Contextual translation English→Japanese contrast cases

1. `The committee approved / the plan / proposed by local engineers`. Weak: `委員会は承認しました / 計画を / 地元の技術者が提案した`; Preferred: `委員会は / 地元の技術者が提案した計画を / 承認しました`. `N:M, full`.
2. `The agency did not say / the bridge was unsafe / only that another inspection was needed`. Keep the negation scope: `当局は / 橋が危険だとは言わず / 再検査が必要だとだけ述べました`. `N:M, partial`.
3. `to 42 clinics / over three years` must become natural particle-marked phrases without separating `42施設に` or `3年間で`.

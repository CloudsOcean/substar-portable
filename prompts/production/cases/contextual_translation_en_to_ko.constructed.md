# Contextual translation English→Korean contrast cases

1. `The committee approved / the plan / proposed by local engineers`. Weak: `위원회가 승인했습니다 / 방안을 / 지역 기술자들이 제안한`; Preferred: `위원회는 / 지역 기술자들이 제안한 방안을 / 승인했습니다`. `N:M, full`.
2. `The agency did not say / the bridge was unsafe / only that another inspection was needed`. Keep the negation scope: `당국은 / 다리가 위험하다고 말한 것이 아니라 / 추가 점검이 필요하다고만 밝혔습니다`. `N:M, partial`.
3. `to 42 clinics / over three years` must become natural particle-marked phrases without separating `42개 병원에` or `3년에 걸쳐`.

# Contextual translation Japanese→English contrast cases

1. `地元の技術者が提案した計画を / 委員会が / 承認しました`. Weak: preserve object–subject–verb order; Preferred: `The committee approved / the plan / proposed by local engineers.` `N:M, full`.
2. `橋が危険だとは / 言いませんでした / 再検査が必要だと述べただけです`. Preferred: `They did not say the bridge was unsafe / they only said / another inspection was needed.` Preserve the negation scope. `N:M, partial`.
3. Recover an English subject only from context. Do not infer gender or identity from omitted Japanese arguments or politeness.

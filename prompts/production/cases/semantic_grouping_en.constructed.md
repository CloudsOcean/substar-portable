# English Cue-boundary constructed cases

These cases demonstrate boundary choices only; never copy their facts or wording. `/` marks a display-Cue boundary.

1. **Keep grammatical atoms intact**: never produce `the / regional office`, `will / begin operations`, `to / complete the review`, `carry / out an inspection`, or `sent to / the committee`.
2. **Preserve scope and quantities**: keep negation and comparison readable, and never split `18 million dollars`, `42 clinics`, `3.5 percent`, dates, or ranges internally.
3. **Separate real discourse acts**: in `Did they approve it / Not yet / We expect a decision tomorrow`, the reply is its own Cue. Confirmed speaker, title, credit, or functional-content changes also force a boundary.
4. **Do not cut at every possible seam**: when the combined span fits and there is no strong pause, prefer `Speaking at the conference earlier today` over `Speaking at the conference / earlier today`, and `The crew is trained and ready to leave` over `The crew is trained / and ready to leave`. The hard limit remains a ceiling, not a fill target.
5. **Recover sentence transitions**: in `the proposal was voted on she then left the room`, prefer `the proposal was voted on / she then left the room`, never `the proposal was voted / on she / then left the room`. The preposition completes the first predicate; the pronoun is the subject of the next sentence.
6. **Reconsider adjacent cuts under the limit**: for `we have been asked by the organizers and local residents to postpone any construction`, a coherent layout under 55 characters is `we have been asked / by the organizers and local residents / to postpone any construction`. Avoid `we have been asked by the organizers / and local residents to postpone / any construction`, which strands the object. Do not require all clauses to fit in one Cue.

The cases show recommended outcomes and common failures; they do not require an analysis trace.

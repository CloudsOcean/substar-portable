# Substar 公司字幕切分风格案例库 v1

用途：供 03A1–03A3 检索和少样本提示。`推荐` 是产品规范化后的正例；`不要` 是旧机器基线或被明确否决的切法。公司历史稿若被标为“兼容案例”，只证明业务允许该结构，不代表字符和标点可以原样照搬。

## A. 强名词短语与限定结构

### A1 完整限定词＋修饰语＋中心词

推荐：

```text
the bright lights and just really
this vibrant energy that's in the city
```

不要：

```text
the bright lights and just really this vibrant
energy that's in the city
```

保护：`this vibrant energy`。

### A2 系动词可作桥接点

推荐：

```text
I think also something that we need to bring up is
the trust in China brands now
```

不要把 `the` 留在上一行。

### A3 比较级/最高级结构

推荐：

```text
We're about to visit one of
the biggest science museums in the world
```

可接受但较弱：

```text
We're about to visit
one of the biggest science museums in the world
```

不要拆开 `biggest science museums`。

### A4 最高级进行时名词短语

推荐：

```text
I mean you have the fastest growing
middle class in the world
```

若 49 上限允许，优先保住 `fastest growing middle class`；不能机械在 `middle` 后切。

### A5 复杂名词短语

推荐：

```text
CATL has an understanding that they want to be
a non-competitive battery manufacturer
```

不要把 `a` 留在上一行。

### A6 地域形容词

推荐：

```text
are now making their way into the storefronts of
North American buyers
```

保护：`North American buyers`。

## B. 固定搭配、专名、缩写和数字

### B1 节目名

推荐：

```text
Hello everyone and welcome
to another exciting episode of Let's Meet
```

不要为了行长更接近拆开 `another exciting episode`。

### B2 城市称谓（公司兼容案例）

```text
Today we are standing
in the Cyber Capital of the world
```

公司稿证明桥接行可以很短；显示时去掉句点。

### B3 产品专名

```text
A groundbreaking feature of Changan's approach
is their first global unmanned sales experience space
```

保持 `Changan`、`unmanned sales experience space` 的内部连续性；只要不超过活动硬上限，就不为追求固定长度而拆开。

### B4 数字量词

推荐：

```text
And immediately I have a delivery fleet in a city
of 36 million people overnight
```

不要拆 `36 million people`。

### B5 枚举原子项

推荐：

```text
Basically we're going to sell to
company A B C D E F G
in China to allow them to get into the market
```

不要写成 `company A B C D E F / G`。

### B6 缩写句界

`U.S.` 不能被句子切分器拆成 `U` 和 `S`。显示轨可输出 `US`，原始轨仍恢复 `U.S.`。

### B7 受控纠错

```text
The 3D AD/8D/ city is unfolding
```

禁止静默把 `AD` 攟成 `8D`。

## C. 平行、列举与呼应结构

### C1 from...to...

```text
Through his channels he shares life in China
with a global audience covering everything
from the cost of living and public transportation
to safety schools and family life
```

可以牺牲第二行的独立性，保住后两行呼应。

### C2 seeing...seeing...and...

```text
this genuine curiosity of people around the world
seeing the Instagram photos
seeing the bright lights
and just really this vibrant energy that's in the city
```

各并列项尽量在同一语法层级起行。

### C3 地域列举

```text
You're in America you're in Canada
you're in the United Kingdom
```

切点与列举项边界一致。

### C4 条件/同构列举

```text
So if you're a manufacturer from Chery
if you're a manufacturer from AITO
you're a manufacturer from Seres
you're a manufacturer from Nio
```

优先保持重复骨架，不因字符平衡破坏同构。

### C5 因果链

```text
so that they don't have to reinvent the wheel
don't have to reinvent R&D on these
```

保护 `reinvent the wheel` 和第二个平行谓语。

## D. 合法桥接与整体最优

### D1 关系从句桥接

```text
This is Brad originally from the United States
who moved to China and now
calls Shenzhen home with his family
```

第二行服务于第三行完整性。

### D2 功能词后的合法边界

```text
So this is what we'll call more of
an upper-scale wholesale market
```

`of` 后并非天然坏边界；下一行完整名词短语更重要。

### D3 公司稿中的系动词桥接

```text
The demand for car buyers now is
what's the onboard entertainment?
```

这是公司稿已经接受的桥接类型。

### D4 允许完整动词后起补语

```text
Now you know the entire society
has slipped to a digital currency
```

不要为了 42 字符在 `a / digital currency` 之间断开。

### D5 整体规划，不贪心

推荐：

```text
But I think you know what's really important Alex
is for more people around the world to really
understand the future of our world
our future of our economy you got to come to China
```

先观察整组最弱行，再决定所有边界，不能只把第一行填到 42。

### D6 公司稿中的弱桥接（兼容但不优先复制）

```text
It's so versatile so innovative and really captures
the attention when driving in the street
```

公司允许动宾跨 cue；产品生成时若存在更自然且合法的整体方案，应优先更自然方案。

## E. 短句、回应与口语

### E1 短回答保持独立

```text
Amazing

Absolutely

Yeah
```

不要为了视觉统一并入相邻长句。

### E2 有语义的话语标记默认保留

```text
Well everyone welcome to China's cyberpunk city
```

`Well` 默认不是水词。

### E3 高置信填充音

```text
It's uh// the CPC 105 celebration
```

### E4 即时重复

```text
I've I've// never seen anything like it
cash dominated the the// society
```

只删除后一个紧邻重复词。

### E5 不自动删 `you know`

```text
You know change is such a big part of the culture here
```

只有人工明确指定时才扩大删除范围。

## F. 标点与显示轨

### F1 当前公司模板按上下行分别处理下标点

原文：

```text
Safety, schools, and family life.
```

上行显示（保留上下标点）：

```text
Safety, schools, and family life.
```

下行显示（逗号转空格、句号删除）：

```text
安全 学校和家庭生活
```

字符计数服从本次 `ACTIVE_OUTPUT_PROFILE`，不得从历史公司稿反推固定算法。当前默认配置对英文计入空格和最终保留的标点，软／硬上限为 50/55；用户修改配置后以运行时数值为准。

### F2 双行都允许上标点

```text
He called it “the Cyber Capital”
他称之为“赛博之都”
```

### F3 问号和感叹号可保留

```text
How are you doing my friend?
Whoa!
```

### F4 元标记不属于显示标点

`uh//` 和 `AD/8D/` 在人工草案可见，最终字幕显示时必须投影掉元标记。

## G. 公司式 N:M 映射（Stage 2）

### G1 两条英文共用一条中文

```text
Today we are standing
in the Cyber Capital of the world
```

两条英文可共同映射到：

```text
此时此刻 我们正置身于“赛博之都”
```

### G2 多条源文共同完成中文表达

公司稿允许上一条译文提前承接下一条信息，或多条源文复用同一译文；这必须通过意义群内的 `N→1/N→M` 映射完成，禁止按源文字符比例切译文。

## H. 机器基线的典型错误

### H1 42 字符目标化

不要：

```text
Now you know the entire society has slipped to a
digital currency
```

问题：上一行被机械填长，下一行失去谓语。

### H2 拆开名词短语

不要：

```text
CATL has an understanding that they want to be a
non-competitive battery manufacturer
```

### H3 拆开枚举原子

不要：

```text
company A B C D E F
G in China
```

### H4 只看字符不看修辞骨架

不要把 `seeing...seeing...and...` 压成若干长度接近、语法层级不一致的行。
